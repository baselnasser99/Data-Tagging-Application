What happens next

Four steps from here to a labelled dataset

  1. Create the projectIts name, its questions and how many people answer each task.
  2. Upload itemsImages and audio clips, one by one or as ZIP files.
  3. Hand out tasks to a groupEvery item × question becomes a task, and each goes to whoever has the fewest. If someone leaves, their unfinished tasks go to the others.
  4. Follow progress and resultsSee who did what and how well people agreed, then download the answers as CSV or JSON.
