Labelling work, shared fairly across your team.

  • An even share for everyone. Each task goes to whoever has the lightest load.
  • People can come and go. When someone leaves, their unfinished work moves to the others.
  • A clean dataset at the end. CSV or JSON, with a score for how well people agreed.

Built as a diploma project at the University of Pécs.
